## Temp store

`tempOrchDir(prefix)` returns `orchDirAt(mkdtempSync(...))`, the helper's documented sole way to mint a typed `OrchDir` (`packages/orch/test/helpers/tempdir.ts:15–18`). `testServices({orchDir})` wires services and their store to that directory, with settings in memory (`packages/orch/test/helpers/services.ts:21–25`); tests seed/use rows through `seedAgent` or related fixtures (`packages/orch/test/helpers/agent.ts:76–100`). Temporary directories are released through `removeTempDir` (`packages/orch/test/helpers/tempdir.ts:43–58`).
